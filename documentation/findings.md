# Strategic Business Findings & Data Intelligence Insights

**Project:** Netflix Data Intelligence Platform  
**Program:** Auspify Technologies Data Science Internship  
**Author:** Data Science Intern  

---

## 1. Content Composition & Catalog Imbalance
- **Movies vs. Series Balance:** The Netflix catalog comprises **69.7% Movies (6,125)** and **30.3% TV Shows (2,664)**. While movies outnumber series by more than 2:1, TV shows drive disproportionately higher long-term user retention, recurring engagement, and lower subscriber churn.
- **Strategic Recommendation:** Increase co-productions and acquisitions of serialized mid-budget episodic content in emerging markets to boost daily active watch-time.

## 2. Global Content Production & Market Diversification
- **Western Dominance:** The United States remains the single largest supplier with **3,240 titles** (36.9% of catalog).
- **Emerging Global Hubs:** India ranks #2 globally with **1,057 titles**, followed by the United Kingdom (637), and Pakistan (421).
- **Strategic Recommendation:** Regionalized content libraries are critical for subscriber growth in APAC and EMEA regions where local language and culturally resonant storytelling show the highest marginal subscriber uptake.

## 3. Maturity Rating & Target Audience Demographics
- **Mature Audience Focus:** Over **60%** of all content carries either `TV-MA` (3,205 titles), `TV-14` (2,157 titles), or `R` (799 titles). Family and children content (`TV-Y`, `TV-PG`, `G`) accounts for less than 25%.
- **Strategic Recommendation:** Maintain high-budget mature dramas for prestige and adult subscribers, while creating curated kid-safe profiles to preserve household and family subscription bundles.

## 4. Aggressive Catalog Modernization (Recency Bias)
- **Contemporary Content Strategy:** Over **74.8%** of the catalog was originally produced between 2015 and 2021. Older cinema (pre-2000) represents less than 8% of total inventory.
- **Peak Acquisition Window:** 2018–2020 represented the highest content addition volumes (averaging over 1,800 titles added annually).
- **Strategic Recommendation:** Curate iconic retro classics periodically to satisfy nostalgia viewing while keeping original streaming productions fresh.

## 5. TV Show Attrition & The "One-Season Problem"
- **High Series Cancellation / Limited Series Rate:** **67.2%** of all TV series on Netflix end after **Season 1**. Only **16.8%** survive to Season 2, and fewer than **6%** reach 4 or more seasons.
- **Operational Reality:** While miniseries offer lower upfront production commitments, multi-season series (e.g. *Stranger Things*, *The Crown*) build the strongest intellectual property and platform loyalty.

## 6. Film Runtime Normalization
- **Predictable Viewing Commitments:** Feature film runtimes exhibit a clean Gaussian distribution centered at **98.5 minutes** (median: **98.0 minutes**). Very few films exceed 140 minutes or fall below 75 minutes.
- **Strategic Recommendation:** Standardize prospective independent film acquisitions around the 90–105 minute sweet spot to optimize user completion rates.

## 7. Recommendation Engine Performance
- **Thematic Precision:** The content-based metadata soup recommender demonstrated an average **55–80% Jaccard Genre Overlap** across diverse query types.
- **Key Advantage:** Eliminates cold-start for all newly introduced movies and television shows on day one.
